# Workflow Reliability Session

Date: 2026-05-06

## Purpose

A Workflow Reliability Session is a focused discovery and diagnostic conversation for one AI-assisted workflow. The session identifies whether Profusion can create value by turning that workflow into reviewable evidence and a bounded receipt.

This is the lead-capture offer. It is not a customer portal, not an audit certification, and not a self-serve software rollout.

## Who It Is For

Teams using AI to produce content, analysis, client-facing work, operational decisions, or engineering output faster than the organization can comfortably review and approve.

The first implementation wedge is AI-assisted content and media governance. Coding-agent and agentic engineering workflows are valid strategic targets, but current public examples for those paths are simulated unless marked otherwise.

## What The Session Covers

- One workflow that already uses, or is about to use, AI.
- The business result the workflow is meant to produce.
- The AI touchpoints in the workflow.
- The human review gates that exist today.
- The artifacts that already prove what happened.
- The artifacts that are missing.
- The risk if the output is wrong, unsupported, or hard to defend.
- The evidence boundary for a first receipt.
- The likely pilot shape, exclusions, and success criteria.

## What The Prospect Should Bring

- A short description of the workflow.
- The owner or accountable team.
- The AI tools involved.
- One representative output.
- Known approval or review bottlenecks.
- Existing artifacts such as prompts, briefs, drafts, QA notes, review comments, approval records, or final outputs.
- The person or role that must approve the work.

## Outputs

- A plain-English workflow map.
- A first-pass evidence boundary.
- A list of review gates and missing artifacts.
- A recommendation on whether a Governed AI Workflow Receipt Pilot is worth scoping.
- A clear statement of what Profusion would not claim or replace.

## Boundaries

Profusion does not replace legal, compliance, engineering, brand, hiring, or executive judgment. The session identifies how to make the process behind AI-assisted work easier to inspect and defend.

Profusion does not claim certification, guaranteed compliance, verified truth, code correctness, autonomous governance, production coding-agent instrumentation, or hiring automation.
