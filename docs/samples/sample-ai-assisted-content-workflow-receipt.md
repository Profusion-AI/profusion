## Workflow Name

AI-Assisted Content Approval

## Business Context

A team uses AI assistance to develop a short-form content asset. The business needs to know what was generated, what artifacts exist, who reviewed the output, and what the receipt does not prove before the asset is used externally.

## Evidence Boundary

Inside this sample receipt:

- content brief
- script or generated content draft
- rendered or finalized asset pointer
- QA report or checklist
- human approval record
- receipt limitation language

Outside this sample receipt:

- universal synthetic-media detection
- identity verification
- liveness verification
- legal or compliance certification
- proof that manipulation did not occur outside the captured workflow
- verified truth of every claim

## Artifacts Captured

| Artifact | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content brief | Defines topic, audience, and risk context | Evidence available |
| Script variant | Records the selected AI-assisted content path | Evidence available |
| Rendered asset | Points to the finalized media artifact | Evidence available |
| QA report | Records go/no-go and review notes | QA checkpoint applied |
| Approval record | Records human approval context | Human review recorded |
| Limitations note | States what the receipt does not prove | Limitations included |

## Review Gates

- QA checkpoint applied before external use.
- Human review recorded by the content owner or delegated reviewer.
- Receipt review remains separate from content approval.

## Supported Claims

- Evidence was available for the declared content workflow.
- AI-assisted artifacts were captured or referenced.
- QA checkpoint status was recorded.
- Human review status was recorded.
- Limitations were included.

## Unsupported Claims

- The receipt does not certify compliance.
- The receipt does not verify the truth of every claim.
- The receipt does not prove universal media authenticity.
- The receipt does not verify identity or liveness.
- The receipt does not replace brand, legal, compliance, or executive approval.

## Recommended Next Controls

- Confirm the approval owner before packet use.
- Review unsupported claims before showing the packet externally.
- Keep receipt approval separate from content approval.
- Use a Workflow Reliability Session to decide whether a paid pilot should capture this workflow with real artifacts.
