# Profusion Offer Positioning

Status: working doctrine
Owner: Kyle
Last updated: 2026-05-17

## Default Offer

Workflow Reliability Session -> Governed AI Workflow Receipt Pilot

One-line public framing:

Profusion helps teams turn one high-risk AI-assisted workflow into reviewable evidence.

## Proof Lanes

Primary near-term proof lane:

- AI-assisted content/media governance
- Closest to Profusion's shipped evidence path
- Best supported by current workflow receipt and content evidence artifacts

Secondary strategic lane:

- AI coding-agent / agentic workflow reliability
- Frame as a Workflow Reliability Session or design-partner diagnostic
- Do not frame as shipped production instrumentation, AI observability, or code correctness tooling

## What The Session Produces

- a selected high-risk AI-assisted workflow
- an evidence boundary
- artifact map
- human review gates
- risk and no-claim notes
- pilot receipt outline
- next safe implementation plan

## Approved Language

- reviewable evidence
- workflow receipt
- evidence boundary
- artifacts and review gates
- what happened and what was reviewed
- what the receipt does and does not prove
- verification tax
- handoff reliability
- governed AI workflow

## Avoid

- platform
- certification
- compliance guarantee
- autonomous governance
- code correctness
- AI observability as a shipped product
- Work Trust as shipped product
- recruiting automation
- hiring recommendations
- candidate ranking
- automated decisioning

## Buyer Hypotheses

Best initial buyers are small enough for founder-led discovery and already feeling AI workflow risk:

- content and communications agencies using AI-assisted media
- AI automation or recruiting-operations consultancies
- AI-forward engineering/product teams adopting coding agents
- governance/risk operators who have policies but lack proof-of-process artifacts
