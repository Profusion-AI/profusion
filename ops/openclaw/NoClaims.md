# Profusion No-Claims List

Status: active guardrail
Owner: Kyle
Last updated: 2026-05-17

## Purpose

This file defines claims OpenClaw and Codex must not make in Profusion outreach, briefs, docs, or draft copy unless Kyle explicitly approves a new evidence-backed claim.

## Do Not Claim

- Profusion is a platform, enterprise platform, certification service, compliance guarantee, AI observability platform, hiring automation system, candidate ranking system, or autonomous governance product.
- Profusion certifies truth, authenticity, legality, compliance, identity, liveness, model correctness, code correctness, security, or absence of manipulation.
- Profusion makes hiring recommendations, ranks candidates, screens candidates, or automates employment decisions.
- Profusion has shipped Work Trust, recruiting automation, production coding-agent instrumentation, AI observability, or a production integration layer.
- Profusion replaces human review, legal judgment, compliance judgment, security review, or management accountability.
- Profusion has customers, integrations, certifications, audits, guarantees, or performance metrics that are not explicitly documented.
- OpenClaw can send or follow up with prospects without Kyle-approved sender identity, campaign, recipient list, copy family, daily cap, suppression/unsubscribe process, and ledger format.

## Allowed Safer Framing

- Profusion helps teams turn one high-risk AI-assisted workflow into reviewable evidence.
- Profusion can help define an evidence boundary, capture artifacts, apply review gates, and produce a plain-English workflow receipt.
- A receipt can show what happened, what artifacts exist, what a human reviewed, and what the receipt does and does not prove.
- The near-term offer is a Workflow Reliability Session followed by a Governed AI Workflow Receipt Pilot.
- For coding-agent work, use diagnostic language: workflow reliability, verification tax, review burden, handoff quality, and evidence design. Do not imply production observability instrumentation is shipped.

## Risk Escalation

Any prospect question about legal compliance, hiring decisions, candidate ranking, certification, guarantees, code correctness, security review, contract terms, or production integrations requires Kyle review before any response is sent.
